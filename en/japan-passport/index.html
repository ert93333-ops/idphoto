Japanese passport photo requirements

The japanese passport preset is 35 mm wide × 45 mm high.

Print size: 35 × 45 mm · 300 dpi.

  • Borderless 35×45mm; crown to chin 34±2mm.
  • Top margin 2–6mm (automatic target 4mm); side margins at least 2mm each.
  • Plain background; white recommended.

Source: Official guidance

Automatic framing estimates head size; verify crown, chin, margins and the authority’s current rules manually. No acceptance guarantee.
