Korean passport photo requirements

The korean passport preset is 35 mm wide × 45 mm high.

Print size: 35 × 45 mm · 300 dpi.

  • Head: crown (top of skull, excluding hair) to chin 32–36mm.
  • Taken within 6 months; uniform white background.
  • Online: recommended 413×531px (accepted width 395–431px × height 507–550px), JPG up to 500KB, 300 dpi.

Source: Official guidance · Online file rules

Automatic framing estimates head size; verify crown, chin, margins and the authority’s current rules manually. No acceptance guarantee.

Use a photo taken against a real white wall. Background removal/compositing or retouching may make the photo unacceptable. Use crop, size and print only: background replacement OFF, retouch 0, lighting/brightness/contrast off.
